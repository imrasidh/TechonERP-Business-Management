# Executive Summary

This document defines the long-term product vision and development roadmap for TechonERP.

The roadmap provides strategic direction for future enhancements while ensuring that the ERP continues to evolve without compromising stability, maintainability, or backward compatibility.

This document is intended for planning purposes and does not represent guaranteed delivery dates.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define

- Long-term product vision
- Planned architectural improvements
- Future business modules
- Technical enhancements
- Scalability objectives
- Integration strategy
- Innovation roadmap

---

# Product Vision

TechonERP aims to become a modern, scalable, modular ERP platform capable of serving businesses ranging from small retail shops to multi-branch enterprises.

The platform should remain:

- Reliable
- Modular
- Secure
- Fast
- Easy to maintain
- Easy to customize

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

Future development should follow these principles.

- Stability before new features.
- Business value first.
- Modular architecture.
- Backward compatibility whenever practical.
- Minimal disruption to existing users.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# Strategic Goals

Long-term goals include

- Expand supported industries.

- Enable cloud services.
- Increase developer productivity.

# Governance

Roadmap changes should be

- Reviewed by the architecture team.
- Prioritized by business value.
- Technically evaluated.
- Documented.
- Approved before implementation.

Strategic planning should remain controlled and transparent.

---

# Developer Guidelines

Developers should

✅ Design new features as independent modules.

✅ Preserve architectural consistency.

✅ Avoid introducing unnecessary dependencies.

✅ Keep documentation synchronized with implementation.

✅ Prioritize maintainability over short-term shortcuts.

---

# Common Mistakes

❌ Adding features without architectural review.

❌ Breaking backward compatibility unnecessarily.

❌ Ignoring scalability during design.

❌ Mixing experimental features with production code.

❌ Expanding modules without documentation.
