# Executive Summary

The Business Requirements Specification (BRS) defines the business objectives, goals, scope, and high-level requirements of TechonERP.

Unlike technical documentation, this document explains **why** the system exists, **what business problems it solves**, and **what value it provides** to organizations.

It serves as the foundation for all functional and technical decisions throughout the software lifecycle.

# Product Vision

TechonERP aims to become a modern, affordable, modular Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform that enables businesses of all sizes to efficiently manage daily operations through a unified system.

The product is designed to reduce manual work, improve accuracy, increase productivity, and provide business owners with real-time operational visibility.

---

# Business Problem Statement

Many small and medium-sized businesses face challenges such as

- Manual record keeping
- Spreadsheet dependency
- Duplicate data entry
- Poor inventory tracking
- Limited reporting
- Human errors
- Slow decision making
- Lack of synchronization between departments
- Inconsistent business processes

TechonERP addresses these challenges through centralized business management.

The primary business goals are

- Centralize business operations

# Business Requirements

The system shall

- Manage customer information.
- Manage supplier information.
- Manage products and services.
- Track inventory.
- Record sales transactions.
- Record purchase transactions.
- Generate invoices.
- Produce reports.
- Manage user accounts.
- Control permissions.
- Synchronize data across devices.
- Maintain audit history.
- Support backups and recovery.
